# E3 Portrait Plan

I will make three portraits for the same friendly character on branches A, B, and C. I will choose one as the default portrait.

## A

I will add 120 small circles around the face in three rings of 40. They will look like a bubble halo and make the character feel lively.

## B

I will draw two separate images myself: a flower and a green leaf. I will save them as transparent PNGs. The flower will go at the top left of the face, and the leaf at the top right.

## C

I will use a light yellow to orange gradient on the face. I will draw a smiling mouth with a Bézier curve to give the character a warm, friendly look.

## Choice

I will choose the portrait that looks clear at a small size and best shows the character's friendly, lively personality.
